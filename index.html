Preparing a Fish Grow-out Facility

An introductory demonstration lesson, not a full curriculum module.

ModulesSample LessonActivitiesQuiz

Why prepare the facility?

A prepared grow-out facility helps maintain suitable conditions for fish and reduces avoidable risks before stocking.

  • Inspect the facility for damage or leaks.
  • Remove debris and unwanted materials safely.
  • Check that water supply and drainage systems work.
  • Follow safety practices and use appropriate protective equipment.
  • Confirm the environment is ready before introducing fingerlings.

Water Quality Management

Healthy water helps fish survive, feed, and grow.

ModulesSample LessonActivitiesQuiz

Why does water quality matter?

Fish depend on their aquatic environment for oxygen, comfort, and survival. Regular monitoring helps prevent stress and disease.

  • Temperature: influences fish metabolism and activity.
  • Dissolved oxygen: is needed for respiration.
  • pH: measures how acidic or basic water is.
  • Ammonia: can build up from waste and uneaten feed.
