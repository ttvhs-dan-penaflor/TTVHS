Introduction to Responsible Fish Capture

Sample Fish Capture lesson for testing audio and automatic completion.

DEMONSTRATION LESSON — for testing text-to-speech and automatic completion. Not an official full lesson.

ModulesSample LessonActivitiesQuiz

What is fish capture?

Fish capture is the activity of catching fish from natural bodies of water, such as rivers, lakes, and seas. It is different from fish culture, which raises fish in managed facilities.

Responsible fish capture considers the safety of fishers, the condition of equipment, and the protection of aquatic resources.

  • Check fishing tools and protective equipment before use.
  • Consider weather and water conditions before a fishing activity.
  • Follow applicable fishing rules and avoid harmful practices.
  • Handle catches carefully and keep the work area clean.

Water Quality Management

Healthy water helps fish survive, feed, and grow.

DEMONSTRATION LESSON — for testing text-to-speech and automatic completion. Not an official full lesson.

ModulesSample LessonActivitiesQuiz

Why does water quality matter?

Fish depend on their aquatic environment for oxygen, comfort, and survival. Regular monitoring helps prevent stress and disease.

  • Temperature: influences fish metabolism and activity.
  • Dissolved oxygen: is needed for respiration.
  • pH: measures how acidic or basic water is.
  • Ammonia: can build up from waste and uneaten feed.

Preparing a Fish Grow-out Facility

Sample lesson for testing the reading voice and automatic completion.

DEMONSTRATION LESSON — for testing text-to-speech and automatic completion. Not an official full lesson.

Before stocking fingerlings

Fish culture involves raising aquatic organisms under managed conditions. Before stocking fingerlings, farmers need to prepare a safe grow-out facility.

A suitable facility provides clean water, enough space, and ways to manage water movement. Poor preparation can cause fish stress or losses.

  • Check ponds or tanks for leaks and damaged parts.
  • Remove trash, harmful objects, and unwanted materials.
  • Ensure there is an adequate, suitable water supply.
  • Prepare tools and practice safety before handling fish.
  • Observe the facility before placing fingerlings into it.

Contemporary and Emerging Arts

Sample Music and Arts reading lesson for Grade 7 Sunflower.

DEMONSTRATION LESSON — for testing text-to-speech and automatic completion. Not an official full lesson.

Art in our community

Contemporary art refers to art created by artists working in the present era. It may use painting, photography, digital tools, performance, or a combination of materials.

Emerging art forms develop as artists experiment with technology, local traditions, and new ways to communicate ideas. Art can tell stories about community life and culture.

  • Observe art in the school, home, and community.
  • Identify the message or idea an artwork communicates.
  • Recognize that artists may use traditional or digital materials.
  • Respect different interpretations of an artwork.
